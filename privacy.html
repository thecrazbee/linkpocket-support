Link Pocket Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Link Pocket handles information when you use the Link Pocket mobile application.

Information you provide

Link Pocket lets you save URLs, titles, notes, tags, and favorites. This content is stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive or operate a server that stores this content.

Information we collect

Link Pocket does not require an account and does not collect personal information, device identifiers, location, contacts, advertising data, analytics, or diagnostic data. The app does not contain advertising or tracking SDKs.

Sharing and exports

When you choose to share a link or export a backup, the app sends the selected content only to the destination you choose through your device's operating-system share or file interface. Link Pocket does not control how that destination handles the content.

Network access

The app may use network access when you open a saved URL. That website or destination may have its own privacy practices. Link Pocket does not automatically upload your saved collection to the developer.

Data retention and deletion

Your Link Pocket data remains on your device until you delete individual entries, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. You can create a portable JSON backup using the Export Backup feature. Deleting the app may permanently remove local data that was not exported.

Children's privacy

Link Pocket is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Because saved content remains on your device, its protection depends in part on your device security. Use a device passcode and keep exported backup files in a secure location.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Link Pocket's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.

Contact

For privacy questions, email linkpocket.support@gmail.com.
