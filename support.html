Frequently asked questions

Where are my links stored?
Your collection is stored locally on your device. Link Pocket does not require an account.

How do I back up my links?
Open Link Pocket, choose More, then select Export Backup. Keep the exported JSON file somewhere safe.

How do I restore a backup?
Choose More → Import Backup and select a Link Pocket JSON backup.

What happens if I uninstall the app?
Uninstalling the app may delete its locally stored data. Export a backup first.
